# Third-party material and rights boundaries

- Original implementation/build/calculation code is MIT. Explanatory prose, reconstructed plots, downloadable generated datasets and archival movies have separate content terms in `LICENSING.md`.
- Historical papers, scans and their original figures remain subject to their own rights. Recomputing a curve from equations does not license the original published figure. Public-source references and citation metadata are not blanket redistribution permissions.
- Archival recordings can contain author/coauthor credits. Keep those credits; this policy does not resolve or transfer joint or institutional rights. No blanket MIT/CC license is assigned to those videos or images.
- Python/NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib and encoding/build dependencies retain their upstream licenses. Using a dependency to generate an output does not by itself put the output under its software license.
